# Editorial Magazine Projects

Use this entry as a source-linked case library for magazine covers, editorial
spreads, long-form visual essays, and publication systems. Preserve the
smallest durable signal: reading rhythm, type/image behavior, format changes,
and reusable principles. Keep external work as inspiration-only unless the
source explicitly grants reuse rights; do not vendor artwork, fonts, videos,
or brand marks.

Each entry records:

- `project`: title, maker, source URL, medium, and format.
- `readingRhythm`: page or screen pacing, hierarchy, and entry points.
- `compositions`: cover, spread, framing, and grid behavior.
- `typeImageSystems`: typography and image relationships.
- `assets`: illustration, photography, marks, and support media.
- `implementation`: format, complexity, and evidence limits.
- `capture`: date, source links, attribution, reuse policy, and capture depth.
- `status`: `lead`, `accepted`, `backfill-needed`, or `archived`.

Use `accepted` only when the source gives enough evidence to preserve the
reusable editorial grammar; use `lead` when the publication still needs a
deeper read.

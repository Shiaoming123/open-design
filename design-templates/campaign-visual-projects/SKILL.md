# Campaign Visual Projects

Use this entry as a source-linked case library for brand campaigns,
promotional images, social visual systems, and static plus dynamic campaign
assets. Preserve the smallest durable signal: art direction, channel behavior,
motion cutdowns, and reusable principles. Keep external work as
inspiration-only unless the source explicitly grants reuse rights; do not
vendor artwork, fonts, videos, or brand marks.

Each entry records:

- `project`: title, maker, source URL, medium, and format.
- `artDirection`: image, color, type, and composition decisions.
- `channels`: static, social, environmental, and screen applications.
- `motionCutdowns`: animation or sequencing evidence and fallback behavior.
- `assets`: photography, type, texture, templates, and support media.
- `implementation`: format, complexity, and evidence limits.
- `capture`: date, source links, attribution, reuse policy, and capture depth.
- `status`: `lead`, `accepted`, `backfill-needed`, or `archived`.

Use `accepted` only when the source gives enough evidence to preserve the
campaign grammar; use `lead` when the campaign still needs a deeper read.
